EMBERFALL

DUEL OF THE FOUR SPARKS

A card duel against Rook, the Ember Fox. Both of you begin with 20 embers of life. Play a card each turn, draw back up to five, and burn Rook's embers to zero before he burns yours.

Flame · strike Wave · mend Leaf · thorn Stone · ward
Surge: play the same suit as your last card and its effect doubles.
Read Rook: he telegraphs the suit he will play next — ward up when Flame is coming, strike while he mends.
Ward: Stone shields you; shield soaks damage before life does.

First to zero embers loses. The deck reshuffles when it runs out.
